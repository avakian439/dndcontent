_**Nervous Impairment.**_ All attacks inflict 1 stack of _Nervous Impairment_. At 6 stacks the target loses their sanity until the end of their next turn, being afflicted with short term madness, during this affliction the creature cannot gain any more stacks of _Nervous Impairment_, afterwards reset stack count to 0. At the end of their turn a creature loses 1 stack of _Nervous Impairment_.

Short-term madness for nervous impairment

Roll d 100. Effect lasts until the end of the affected creature's next turn.

| d 100  | Effect                                                                                                                      |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01–20  | The creature retreats into its mind and becomes paralyzed. The effect ends if the creature takes any damage.                |
| 21–30  | The creature becomes incapacitated and spends the duration screaming, laughing, or weeping.                                 |
| 31–40  | The creature becomes frightened and must use its action and movement to flee from the source of the fear.                   |
| 41–50  | The creature begins babbling and is incapable of normal speech or spellcasting.                                             |
| 51–60  | The creature must use its action to attack the nearest creature.                                                            |
| 61–70  | The creature experiences vivid hallucinations and has disadvantage on ability checks.                                       |
| 71–75  | The creature does whatever anyone tells it to do that isn't obviously self-destructive.                                     |
| 76–80  | The creature is overwhelmed by a crushing sense of the depths: its speed is halved and it has disadvantage on attack rolls. |
| 81–90  | The creature is stunned.                                                                                                    |
| 91–100 | The creature falls unconscious.                                                                                             |
