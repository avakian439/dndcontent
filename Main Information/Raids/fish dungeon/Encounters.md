# Encounter 1 — The Antiphon

> [!abstract] Summary
> A flooded cave gated by a pink barrier. Three crystals must be shattered to dissolve it, but each crystal is only breakable while its **litany** is satisfied — ring the four correct [[The Bells|bells]] in the spoken order before the sanctuary lapses. Six bells, four right, two decoys. **No wipe.** Fail the order and all three crystals reset.
>
> This is where the party first meets the hive's written language ([[The Abyssal Script]]) — the same decode skill the [[Bishop Quintus|boss room]] later tests.

**Related:** [[The Abyssal Script]] · [[The Bells]] · [[Nervous Impairment]] · [[Bishop Quintus]]

---

## The Room

A drowned cavern. The party enters from the north through a **10-ft-high ledge** (a drop in, non-trivial to climb back out — no easy retreat).

| Map element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pink barrier (north wall) | Seals the way forward. Dissolves when all 3 crystals break. |
| 3 pink crystals | The locks. Each breakable only while its litany is active. |
| 6 bells (green) | Glyph-bells. Glyphs *appear in light* above them per crystal. |
| 2 black masses | Rock pillars — full cover, block line of sight to bells behind them. |
| Blue pool | Standing water. Difficult terrain (DM's call on depth). |

> [!info] No ambient Song here
> Nervous Impairment is **only** applied by enemy actions/effects. There are no enemies in this room, so no stacks accrue — the pressure is the **timer**, not the Song. The bells still project sanctuaries; here that's mechanical flavor (nothing to shelter from yet).

---

## Objective

Break all **3 crystals** → the barrier dissolves → the path forward opens. That's the whole encounter.

---

## Core Mechanics

> [!note] The bells
> - Each bell can be **activated at range** by attacking it (**action or bonus action**), or **for free** if a character is **adjacent**.
> - Activating a bell raises its **sanctuary** and starts a **3-round timer** (the sanctuary's duration).
> - When the timer lapses, **all bells deactivate** and the attempt ends.

> [!note] The litany (per crystal)
> - When the party focuses a crystal, **glyphs appear in light above the six bells** (a fresh set) and the hive **sings the sequence** — spoken aloud as an ordered list of *meanings*.
> - The sequence always names **four** glyphs. The **other two bells are distractors.**
> - To read it, the party must know which glyph means which word — i.e. they must decode [[The Abyssal Script]]. Nothing hands them the key; they work it out by doing.

> [!warning] Solving a crystal
> 1. Ring **bell #1 ** of the sequence. The **3-round clock** starts.
> 2. Ring the remaining three correct bells **in order**, before the clock runs out.
> 3. While all four are lit in the correct order, the crystal is **breakable** — any hit shatters it.
> 4. Repeat for the next crystal (new glyphs, new sequence).

---

## Failure & Reset

> [!danger] Break the sequence, lose everything
> - Ring a **wrong bell** (a distractor, or out of order) → the litany fails and **all three crystals reset to unbroken.**
> - **No wipe** — no one dies, no damage. The cost is *progress*, not blood.

> [!tip] Optional softeners (DM's call)
> The full reset-to-3 is punishing for a no-death room. If your table sours on it, consider:
> - **Per-crystal reset:** a wrong bell only resets the *current* crystal, keeping already-broken ones broken.
> - **Timer lapse ≠ wrong sequence:** if the clock simply runs out (no wrong bell rung), let the bells deactivate and retry *without* resetting broken crystals.
> - **Ring-to-reveal (learning aid):** ringing a bell briefly *sings its meaning*, so the room teaches the alphabet through experimentation. Keeps "nothing hands them a key" true while making the cold-start decode humane. Recommended for the *first* crystal at least.
