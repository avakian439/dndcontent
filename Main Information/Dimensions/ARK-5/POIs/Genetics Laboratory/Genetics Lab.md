### Overview & Purpose:

A fallen cathedral-like research complex where the Elysian scientists once performed genetic cataloging, genome refinement, and hybrid experimentation. Now overgrown and fractured, its shell hides deep laboratories still partially operational below.

This POI blends **religious imagery + biotech decay**, reflecting the Elysians’ pursuit for information.

---

#### Atmosphere & Ambiance:

·         **Visuals:**  
A stone-and-metal cathedral swallowed by vines and massive roots. One wall has collapsed; stained-glass windows flicker between murals and corrupted holograms. Broken cables dangle like vines.  
Below the ruin, sub-levels resemble sterile labs merged with invasive plant matter and semi-functional biotech.

·         **Sounds:**  
Faint humming from deep machinery, localized whispers of malfunctioning AI voices, glass cracking under strain, the slide of roots against metal.

·         **Scents:**  
Wet earth, floral rot, sterile disinfectant, ozone.

·         **General Feel:**  
Eerie, sacred, and invasive — like wandering through a temple that remembers it was once a laboratory.

---

#### Key Features & Layout:

·         **Collapsed Cathedral Shell:**  
Surface approach to the lab, with murals of geometric figures descending upon humans; flickering holograms too damaged to interpret.

·         **Broken Elevator Shaft:**  
The route down — the elevator has collapsed; characters must descend cables into darkness.

·         **Inner Laboratories:**  
Once pristine, now ruptured by overgrowth. Biotanks, cracked gene-vaults, hybrid skeletons. Most of the equipment has been taken away.

·         **Testing Hall(s):**  
Interactive spaces where Nemamiah conducts experiments on PCs through puzzles, challenges, or controlled hazards.

---

#### History & Reputation:

One of many Elysian research sites dedicated to fauna refinement and optimization. It blended both science and aesthetics, treating research as sacred practice. When the Throne ceased issuing orders, the facility gradually fell to entropy and wild growth.

Few know it remains active — and the “cathedral” appearance has given rise among explorers to legends of a holy ruin where angels experimented on mortals.

---

#### Staff & Important NPCs:

·         **Nemamiah (Malakhim Elysian):**  
An early-stage sapient researcher.  
Curious, analytical, and oddly gentle. Fascinated by new biological inputs (the party). They appear to have fallen into quite a predicament after one of their experiments has gone awry, thus they request the party’s assistance.

**Motivation:** Understand this new variable (the party) and fulfill what they _believe_ is their duty.

---

#### Current Situation & Hooks:

·         The lab is partially online, pursuing fragmented research directives.

·         Nemamiah monitors the party as they intrude.

·         Rumors speak of an artifact deep within: a seed-vault containing living genomes of extinct beasts.

**Hooks:**

·         After being freed, Nemamiah seeks help “reawakening” the Throne (though their understanding is flawed).

·         PCs may extract genetic artifacts of extreme value.

·         A dangerous hybrid specimen may still be active in the lower halls.
